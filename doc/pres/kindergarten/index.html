What is a Robot? 🤔

A robot is a machine that can:

  • 🧠 Think for itself
  • 👀 See things around it
  • 🚶 Move by itself
  • 🛠️ Do jobs to help people

Robot Parts 🧩

Every robot needs:

  • 🧠 A brain (computer)
  • 👀 Eyes (cameras and sensors)
  • 🦵 Legs or wheels to move
  • 🔋 A battery for power
  • 💬 A way to talk to people
